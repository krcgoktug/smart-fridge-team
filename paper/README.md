## Submission target

- **TechRxiv** (https://www.techrxiv.org) — preprint, free, ~1–3 days
  to indexed DOI, IEEE-branded.
- After community feedback, the manuscript will be re-targeted to an
  IEEE conference (likely IEEE IoT Magazine or IEEE BigData Workshop)
  or IEEE Access.

## Authoring discipline

- Original schematics and diagrams will be generated by the authors for
  every figure; no photographs taken by non-authors are reproduced in
  the manuscript.
- Real sensor data, mobile-app screenshots, and source-code excerpts
  appearing in the manuscript are drawn from the authors' own
  development environment, captured during demonstration sessions and
  archived in this repository under `paper-assets/`.
- An Author Contributions section explicitly attributes the work
  according to the project's git history.
